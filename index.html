About Electroreck

Where Engineers
Make Wonders Happen

Electroreck is the official electrical engineering society of NIT Kurukshetra — an aggregation of like-minded engineers connected through a shared vision of technical excellence and innovation.

As a society, we proudly conduct Techspardha — the annual techno-cultural fest of NIT KKR at the national level, attracting a footfall of over 10,000 participants every year.

Under the guidance of our Faculty In-Charge Dr. Sandeep Kakran, our members conduct technical workshops, research seminars, and hands-on sessions that prepare engineers to be future-ready.
